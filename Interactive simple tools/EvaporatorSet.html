Estimated external steam users

Estimates use 450 lb steam/hr per ton cane/hr for pans and 270 lb steam/hr per ton cane/hr for juice heaters. Tons of inlet juice per hour are assumed equal to tons cane per hour.

Steam grade matters. Factories commonly split juice heating between primary and secondary heaters using different vapor grades—for example V2 and V1, respectively. Secondary heaters need steam hot enough to raise juice above its flash point. Pans may use V1, V2 and potentially V3 depending on pan type, heating surface and the evaporation scheme. The supply-steam temperature and massecuite boiling temperature must be investigated to confirm adequate boiling rate and production.
